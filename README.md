## About the Company

**CV. YTCRAFT INDONESIA** is an Indonesian technology trading and service company. We operate at the intersection of product supply and technical delivery: sourcing and distributing computer hardware, electronics, laboratory and healthcare equipment, while building the software and digital solutions that make that equipment useful in daily operations.

Our business spans several disciplines — software and IT solutions, electronics and technology equipment, printing and creative production, and digital product development. This range is deliberate. Organisations rarely need a single item; they need a supplier who understands how hardware, software, and support fit together, and who remains accountable after delivery.

We work with a straightforward principle: recommend what the requirement actually calls for, specify it honestly, deliver it properly, and support it afterwards. As an Indonesian company, we are building capability at home — engineering talent, service standards, and partnerships — with the intention of competing on quality in national and international markets.

## Technology &amp; Innovation

Our technology direction is organised into two distinct tracks, so that what we offer today is never confused with what we are building toward.

### Delivered through our services

- **Software development** — web and desktop applications, internal business systems, and integration work.
- **IT infrastructure** — hardware specification, procurement, installation, and configuration.
- **Equipment supply** — electronics, laboratory, and healthcare equipment sourced to requirement.
- **Printing &amp; creative production** — design, print, and finishing for corporate needs.
- **Technical support** — maintenance, troubleshooting, and after-sales service.

### Development focus

These areas represent our research and development interest. They are described as direction, not as commercially deployed services.

| Area | Direction |

| **Artificial Intelligence** | Applied AI for document processing, operational insight, and decision support. |
| **Internet of Things** | Sensor integration, device connectivity, and remote monitoring. |
| **Automation Systems** | Process automation for repetitive and measurement-driven workflows. |
| **Cloud Technology** | Cloud-hosted systems, deployment, and managed infrastructure. |
| **Digital Transformation** | Modernising paper-based and disconnected operational processes. |
| **Digital Management Solutions** | Platforms for assets, inventory, and organisational data. |

<a id="goals"></a>

## Strategic Goals

1. **Build an integrated portfolio.** Connect product supply, software, and technical service so clients can source related needs through a single accountable partner.
2. **Establish dependable supply and quality assurance.** Develop sourcing channels and acceptance processes that make product quality predictable rather than accidental.
3. **Strengthen in-house engineering capability.** Grow practical competence in software development, IoT integration, and applied AI within the company.
4. **Expand institutional collaboration.** Build working relationships with industry, educational institutions, research laboratories, and public organisations.
5. **Institutionalise governance and service standards.** Document processes, maintain service records, and operate in a way that supports long-term growth.
